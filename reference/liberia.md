# River-blindness in Liberia

Counts of reported onchocerciasis (or riverblindess) cases from 90
villages sampled across Liberia.

## Usage

``` r
data(liberia)
```

## Format

A data frame with 90 rows and 6 variables:

- lat:

  latitude of the of sampled villages.

- long:

  longitude of the sampled villages.

- ntest:

  number of tested people for the presence nodules.

- npos:

  number of people that tested positive for the presence of nodules.

- elevation:

  the elevation in meters of the sampled village.

- log_elevation:

  the log-transformed elevation in meters of the sampled village.

Zouré, H. G. M., Noma, M., Tekle, Afework, H., Amazigo, U. V., Diggle,

Levels and Estimated Number Infected. *Parasites & Vectors*, 7, 326.
